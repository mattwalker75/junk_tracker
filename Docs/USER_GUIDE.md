# Craving Crusher — user guide

## The first time
Open the app (`./TRACKER.sh` opens http://localhost:8420). There is nothing to set up: it opens
straight onto your log, with no account and no password. You are called "Me" until you tap the
pencil at the top of Settings and type your name.

Want other people on it, or a password? Settings → **Users and sign-in** (below).

## Home
- **My goal** — when you have a goal, it sits at the very top on a yellow sticky note, so you see
  it every time you open the app. Under it: which day of the goal you are on and how it is going.
  Tap **Done** when you have done it — the next goal on your list takes its place (and **Undo** is
  there for a moment if you tapped it by mistake). When a goal's time is up, the note asks
  *did you do it?*: **Yes, done**, or **Keep going** to leave it up with no end date. Tap the
  words to open your goals.
- **Days clean** — whole days since your last splurge. `0` means you had one today. Under it:
  the day and, if you said, where.
- **This week** — splurges since the start of the week (Sunday or Monday, your choice in Settings).
- **30 days** — splurges in the last 30 days, and how many were feasts.
- **The last splurge** — its size, what it was and how long ago. Tap it to open that day.
- **Activity this week** — how many of this week's 7 days you were active (the week starts on
  Sunday or Monday, your choice in Settings), the minutes and calories burned this week (the
  calories only from activities where you filled them in), your last activity (tap it to open
  that day), and a dot for each day of the week — green when you were active. Tap the card for
  the week in detail: days active, time and calories burned, each kind of activity (how many
  times, how long), and the seven days one by one with every activity and its time. Tap a day there
  to open it (that is where you change or delete an activity); the arrows go to earlier weeks.
- **Last 14 days** — one square a day, coloured by the biggest splurge that day:
  pale = Snack, orange = Meal, red = Feast, grey = clean. A short green bar under the date means
  you were active that day (on the orange and red squares the bar is white, or dark in a dark
  theme, so it still shows). Today has an outline. Tap any day to see what you had, what you did
  and your weigh-in, to change or delete them, or to add something to that day. Tap anywhere
  else on the card to open the **Calendar**, which goes further back.
- **Weight** — your latest weigh-in, how much it went up or down over your trend period
  (green when down), the average of the last 7 days, and a small line of the period.
- **+ Splurge**, **+ Activity** and **Weigh in** — the three big buttons at the bottom.

## Log a splurge
1. **What did you have?** — or tap one under *Again?* (the last few different things you had;
   it fills in the size too).
2. **How big a splurge?** — **Snack**, **Meal** or **Feast**. Under each is an example: yours
   from Settings, or a candy bar / burger & fries / a whole pizza. Pick the closest.
3. Optional: **Where** and **Calories**.
4. **Day** — **Today** unless you tap **Yesterday**, or **Other day** for a big calendar of the
   month (arrows for earlier months; days after today can't be picked).
5. Optional: **Time** — empty to start; tap **Now** for the time right now, or pick one; **Clear**
   takes it off again. The tracker doesn't need it; in game mode a few monsters care about the time
   of day (see *Calorie Crusher* below), and with no time they assume the worst.
6. Optional: **Planned treat** — switch it on for a treat you planned ahead (a birthday dinner, a
   night out). It is still a splurge and counts on Home like any other; in game mode it heals the
   monster only half as much.
7. Optional: **Notes**.
8. **Save splurge**.

To change or delete one, tap its day on Home or in the Calendar, then tap **Edit** on the splurge.

## Log activity
1. **What did you do?** — type it, tap one under *Again?* (your last few different activities,
   with how long they were; it fills in the minutes and calories too), or tap one of the six
   under **Or pick one** (Walk, Run, Bike, Gym, Swim, Yoga — or your own, from Settings).
2. Optional: **How long** — minutes (`90`) or hours and minutes with a colon (`1:30`); the line
   under the box shows what it will save — and **Calories burned**, a whole number.
3. **Day** — the same as a splurge's: **Today**, **Yesterday** or **Other day**. **Time** starts
   at the time you opened the sheet; change it or clear it.
4. Optional: **Notes**.
5. **Save activity**.

Log as many activities a day as you like. To change or delete one, tap its day, then **Edit** or
**Delete** on the activity (Delete asks once more).

## A day
Tap a day on Home or in the Calendar. At the top: the day, how long ago, and how long you were
active. Then the **Splurges** (with the day's calories beside the heading, when you filled any
in), the **Activity** (with the calories burned beside the heading, when you filled any in), the
**Weigh-in**, and **+ Splurge** / **+ Activity** to add one to that day. The arrows go to the day
before or after.

## Weigh in
Type your weight (pounds or kilograms, as set in Settings) and **Save weigh-in**. One weigh-in a
day: if the day already has one, the sheet says so and saving replaces it. The **Day** works like a
splurge's: **Today** unless you tap **Yesterday**, or **Other day** for the big calendar — to fill
in a day you missed. **Remove this day's weigh-in** deletes it. Weigh-ins are optional;
the weight card just waits for the first one.

## Goals
Tap your initials (top right of Home), then **Goals**.
- **Now** — the goal on your Home screen, with **I did it — check it off**.
- **Up next** — the goals waiting, in order. The arrows move one up or down; moving one to the top
  makes it the goal now. A goal's days start when it reaches the top, so goals waiting don't run
  down (and one moved off the top starts over when it comes back).
- **Add a goal** — type it (up to 300 characters — a counter under the box shows how many), or tap
  one of the ideas; choose how long (no end, 1 week, 2 weeks or 1 month) and whether it is the goal
  now or goes at the end of the list. The sticky note writes a short goal big and a longer one
  smaller, so it always fits.
- **Done** — the goals you checked off, with the day.

Tap any open goal to change its words or length, or to delete it. Each person has their own goals.

## Calendar
Any month: days coloured like Home, the green bar under active days, a small dot in the corner of
days with a weigh-in, then the month's
splurge count, clean days and weight change, and the list of that month's splurges. Arrows move
between months. Tap a day to open it.

## Calorie Crusher (game mode)
A monster battle made from your own log. Workouts hit the monster, splurges feed it, and each
monster has a deadline. It is just for fun: **the game never changes your log.** Everything you
log in the game is a normal entry in the tracker, and the tracker looks and works the same whether
you play or not.

**Opening it.** Tap your initials (top right) → **Game mode**. In the game the same menu has
**Back to tracker**. The first time, tap **Start a run** and pick **Normal** or **Hardcore**
(below). Each person has their own run.

**Between fights.** When you beat a monster (or one strikes), the next one does **not** start by
itself: the battle screen says *No monster right now* and shows who is up next. Until it starts,
what you log hits and heals nothing (it still earns +5 points). Start it when you're ready with
**Start the next fight** (it takes you to Choose your fight, where you pick the deadline). Started
on the day you beat the one before, it gets the rest of that day as a head start and its deadline
counts from tomorrow; started on another day, that day is day 1. To have it start by itself each
morning, turn on **Keep the monsters coming** in Settings → You.

**The battle screen** shows your hearts, the run number, your score, the deadline counting down,
the monster with its HP bar and its special trick, a **So close!** line (how many minutes or
calories would finish it), your power-ups and the battle log — newest at the top, in the order
things happened (an entry's time, or, with no time, when you logged it). The two big buttons at the bottom are
**+ Activity (attack)** and **+ Splurge** — the same forms as in the tracker, with the same fields,
plus a live panel that shows what the entry will do before you save it. There is no Weigh in
button in the game; weigh in from the tracker as usual.

**Attacks (your workouts).**
- Damage = **minutes + calories burned ÷ 10**. A 45-minute walk burning 210 calories does
  45 + 21 = **66** damage. So 10 minutes = 100 calories = 10 damage. There is no cap.
- **Comeback:** the first workout after a day with no activity does **double** damage.
- **Clean day:** every day that ends with no splurge is a **critical hit of 25**, landed at midnight.
- A workout with no minutes and no calories does no damage, but still earns its points.

**Heals (your splurges).** A **Snack heals 10**, a **Meal 25**, a **Feast 60**. A **planned treat**
heals half. Some monsters change these numbers (each one's trick is on the battle screen and in
the **Monster Book**).

**Bonus HP.** A splurge still counts when the monster is at full HP: whatever doesn't fit goes on
as **bonus HP**, a violet bar laid over the orange one. It holds up to **20% of the monster's max
HP** (20 for Crumbs, who has 100). Your workouts knock the bonus HP off first, so "So close!" and
the live panels count it in the HP you still have to beat. With full HP and a full bonus bar, a
splurge heals nothing more (the size buttons say *maxed out*).

**Deadlines and the bet.** Each monster must go down by midnight at the end of its last day (the
first day counts as day 1; a monster that starts right after a knockout gets the rest of that day
free). On **Choose your fight** you can bet on a shorter deadline for more points: **Steady** (×1),
**Bold** (one day less, ×1.5) or **Daredevil** (two days less, ×2). The bet can change only before
your first hit lands, on the fight's first day.

**Hearts, strikes, knock-back and defeat.**
- A run starts with **3 hearts**; you can hold up to **5**.
- If the deadline passes and the monster still has HP, it **strikes**: you lose a heart, it heals
  back to full and you fight it again with a fresh deadline.
- You win a heart back by beating a **boss** (every 10th level) and for every week with **5 or
  more active days** (your own week start, from Settings).
- At **0 hearts**, what happens depends on the run's mode (next).

**Normal or Hardcore.** You pick when the run starts.
- **Normal:** at 0 hearts you are **knocked back** to the first level of your world, lose 250
  points, get 3 hearts again, and the run carries on.
- **Hardcore:** at 0 hearts you are **defeated**: the run is over and its score goes into the Hall
  of Heroes. In return every fight's points are worth ×1.25, and the run gets a skull badge.
- A Hardcore run can switch to Normal at any time (tap the mode badge on the map). It can't go back,
  and the Hall shows it as "Hardcore → Normal".

**Power-ups.** You can hold up to 3 of each. Tap one on the battle screen, then tap again to use it.
- **Veggie Shield** — blocks the next heal. Earned with **3 clean days in a row**. If you delete the
  splurge it blocked, the shield comes back.
- **Water Potion** — **50 damage** at once. Earned with a workout of **60 minutes or more**.
- **Second Wind** — **one more day** on the deadline. Earned by beating a boss.
- One monster (Bacon Bandit) doesn't let you use power-ups in its fight; you keep them for later.

**Points.**
- A monster beaten: the damage you did + the monster's full HP + **50 for each day left** on the
  deadline + **100** if no heal landed (flawless) + **500** for a boss, all times your bet (and ×1.25
  on Hardcore).
- A monster that struck you: the damage you did.
- **Every entry you log during the run** (activity, splurge or weigh-in): **+5**, even a Feast — so
  being honest always pays.
- Each knock-back: −250.

**Planned treat and the splurge Time** are the same switches as in the tracker. A few monsters read
the time of a splurge (the Midnight Snacker cares about after 9 PM, Hash Hound about before 11 AM,
Fondue Fiend about splurges close together). If you leave Time empty, they assume the worst.

**What happened while you were away.** When you open the game, it catches up on everything since
you last looked: a **Victory** for each knockout, a **Strike** for each missed deadline, then
**Knocked back** or **Defeated** if a heart ran out. They come one after another; **Next** shows the
next one. If you close the app in the middle, they come back the next time you open the game. A
workout logged from the tracker counts too: the next time you open the game, you see the Victory.

**Finished fights are frozen.** Once a monster is beaten (or has struck), that fight's result stays
as it was, even if you later edit or delete an entry from those days. Changing an entry inside the
fight going on now changes it right away. An entry logged later on the day of a knockout counts for
the next monster if you start it that day.

**The other screens.** The **Adventure map** shows all 60 levels in six worlds (tap a monster for
its card, or a world's sign to see it up close). The **Hall of Heroes** lists everyone's runs (All
time, This month, My runs, Hardcore) and your badges. **How to crush** has every rule and number.
The **Monster Book** has all 60 monsters and their tricks.

**If the game says "Your phone's date looks wrong"**, the phone's date is more than two days away
from the computer running Craving Crusher. Fix the date on the phone and try again.

## Settings
Tap your initials at the top right of Home, then **Settings**. The same menu has **Goals**, and
— with users and sign-in on — **Sign out**.

**Your splurge sizes** — what a Snack, a Meal and a Feast mean *to you* (for example
"candy bar, 2 cookies"). They show under the size buttons when you log. Leave one empty for the
usual example.

**Your quick picks** — the six buttons under *Or pick one* when you log an activity. Write the
things you do most (Hike, Peloton, Tennis…). Leave a box empty for the usual one (Walk, Run,
Bike, Gym, Swim, Yoga).

**When the app opens** — **Tracker** (Home) or **Calorie Crusher** (the game). It only decides
where you land when you open the app; the menu under your initials still switches any time, and a
bookmarked page opens where it points.

**Keep the monsters coming** — off to start with. With it on, the first time you open game mode
each day, if no monster is going, the next one starts by itself with the whole day ahead (a note at
the top says so). It never starts one on the same day a monster went down — start that one yourself
if you want to keep going. If your run is over (or you never started one), a new run starts the
same way, with Crumbs, in the same mode as your last run (Normal for your first); when a run ends
you see how it went first, and the new run starts the next day.

**Weight and weeks** — pounds or kilograms (weigh-ins are kept exactly; only the display
changes); the trend over 1 week, 2 weeks or a month; weeks start on Sunday or Monday.

These are **yours** — each person has their own.

**Users and sign-in** — off to start with: no sign-in, one log. Turn on *Several users, each
signing in* and it asks for your name and a password; you become the first user and your log
stays as it is. From then on everyone signs in, and the **Users** list appears in Settings.
*Stay signed in for* 1 day to 90 days (after a restart). To turn it off, remove everyone but
yourself first (so no log is lost), then type TURN OFF; your password is removed.

**Users** (only with users and sign-in on) — everyone with a log.
- **Add a user**: a name and a password (twice).
- **Password**: set a new password for anyone. If sign-in is on, that person is signed out
  everywhere and uses the new one. Changing your own keeps you signed in here.
- **Switch to**: shows that person's log (and their settings) so you can look or fill it in for
  them. A strip at the top says whose log you are in, with a button to go back to yours.
- **Remove**: deletes the person with all their splurges, activities, weigh-ins and goals (type DELETE to confirm).
  You cannot remove yourself or the last person.

Anyone can do all of this — users keep logs apart; they are not private from each other.

**Appearance** — Light, Dark, System (follows the phone), or **New theme…** to make your own
with a live preview (including the goal note's paper and writing). The same for everyone.

**Network access** — *Allow other devices on my network* lets phones open it (after
`./TRACKER.sh --restart`); the addresses to use are listed. *Other names for this computer* is
for a VPN name such as Tailscale's `my-mac.tailnet-name.ts.net` (applies at once). **Port**
changes the address (after a restart).

**About** — the version, and where the settings file and the database are.

## Running it
| | |
|---|---|
| `./TRACKER.sh` | start it in the background and open it |
| `./TRACKER.sh --stop` | stop it |
| `./TRACKER.sh --restart` | after a setting that says "after a restart" |
| `./TRACKER.sh --status` | is it running, and where? |
| `./TRACKER.sh --logs` | watch the server log (Ctrl-C to leave) |
| `./TRACKER.sh --check` | is everything it needs in place? |
| `./TRACKER.sh --stop`, `git pull`, `./TRACKER.sh --start` | update to new code — the start installs and builds what it needs |
| `./INSTALL_APP.sh -k` | the same by hand: packages and build, no tests |

## Backing up
Everything is in one file, `data/junk.db` (Settings → About shows where). Stop the app and copy
that file, or copy it while running together with `junk.db-wal` if it is there.

When an update changes how the database is laid out, the app first saves a copy of it as it was,
for example `data/junk.db.before-v3` (the update that added activity). Keep it until you are happy with the update.
